Apple TV · tvOS

在 Apple TV 上设置 ChannoSet up Channo on Apple TV

Channo 是 Apple TV 上的播放器。用 Siri Remote 浏览你自己的 M3U 或 Xtream，搜索频道，并查看节目单。电视上同样没有内置频道。Channo on Apple TV plays your own M3U or Xtream source. Use the Siri Remote to browse, search and open the guide. The TV app does not include channels either.

直接在电视上添加Add a source on the TV

  1. 在 Apple TV 的 App Store 安装 Channo，并使用与手机相同的 Apple ID 购买或恢复 Pro（如果需要）。
  2. 添加 M3U 链接，或按 Xtream 登录填写服务器、用户名和密码。
  3. 电视上要输入长地址时，用旁边 iPhone 或 iPad 的连续互通键盘粘贴，避免用遥控器逐字输入。
  4. 免费版最多 3 个播放列表，节目单查看现在和接下来。Pro 解锁无限列表、全天节目单，以及全部来源的 iCloud 同步。
  1. Install Channo from the Apple TV App Store. Use the same Apple ID when you buy or restore Pro.
  2. Add an M3U link, or follow Xtream login and enter the server, username and password.
  3. For a long address, paste with the Continuity Keyboard from a nearby iPhone or iPad instead of typing on the remote.
  4. The free version holds 3 playlists and shows Now and Next. Pro unlocks unlimited playlists, the full-day guide, and iCloud sync for every source.

从 iPhone 同步到 Apple TVSync from iPhone to Apple TV

  1. 两台设备登录同一个 iCloud 账号。
  2. 在 iPhone 的「设置 → iCloud 同步」中打开同步。这个开关默认关闭。
  3. 免费同步第 1 个来源及其收藏和点播进度。Pro 同步全部来源。直播播放到哪一帧不会同步。
  4. Xtream 如果在电视上还没有密码，按提示输入一次。之后用遥控器搜索、打开节目单并播放。
  1. Sign both devices into the same iCloud account.
  2. On iPhone, turn on Settings, then iCloud sync. The switch is off by default.
  3. Free sync covers the first source with its favorites and on-demand progress. Pro syncs every source. The exact live playback position does not sync.
  4. If Apple TV does not have the Xtream password yet, enter it once when asked. Then search, open the guide and play with the remote.

来源支持回看时，正在播出的节目可以从头看。回看使用来源自己的地址，Channo 不在 Apple TV 上录制。When the source supports catch-up, a programme on air can be watched from the beginning. Catch-up uses the source's own addresses. Channo does not record on Apple TV.

电视上没有的功能Not on the TV

离线下载、画中画和 CarPlay 属于 iPhone / iPad。Apple TV 用来在大屏上播放。使用情况与故障报告可在电视的 Pro 页面关闭。手机上的添加步骤见 M3U 导入 和 使用指南。Offline downloads, Picture in Picture and CarPlay belong to iPhone and iPad. Apple TV plays on the big screen. Usage and fault reporting can be turned off on the TV's Pro screen. For adding sources on the phone, see M3U setup and the setup guide.

常见问题Questions

必须先在 iPhone 上添加播放列表吗？Do I have to add the playlist on iPhone first?

不必。可以在 Apple TV 上直接添加 M3U 链接。也可以先在 iPhone 上添加，再打开 iCloud 同步，让来源出现在电视上。No. You can add an M3U link directly on Apple TV. You can also add it on iPhone first, then turn on iCloud sync so the source appears on the TV.

为什么 Apple TV 还要再输入一次 Xtream 密码？Why does Apple TV ask for the Xtream password again?

播放列表同步不会携带独立保存的密码。密码在系统钥匙串里。电视上还没有这项钥匙串记录时，Channo 会再问一次。Playlist sync does not carry a password stored separately. The password is in the system Keychain. If the TV does not have that Keychain item yet, Channo asks once.

Apple TV 上的节目单为什么只有现在和接下来？Why does the Apple TV guide show only Now and Next?

免费版查看现在和接下来。全天节目单属于 Pro。节目单数据来自你自己的 XMLTV 或来源，Channo 不提供节目单服务。Now and Next are free. The full-day guide is part of Pro. Guide data comes from your own XMLTV or source. Channo does not provide a guide service.

能在 Apple TV 上下载影片吗？Can I download movies on Apple TV?

不能。离线下载在 iPhone 和 iPad 上，而且只针对符合条件的点播，不录制直播。No. Offline downloads are on iPhone and iPad, and only for eligible on-demand video. Channo does not record live TV.
