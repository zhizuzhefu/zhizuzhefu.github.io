Listen through CarPlay. Parked video requires a compatible system and vehicle that permits video; driving uses audio only. Where supported, free parked video is limited to 20 minutes per day. Pro removes that time limit, subject to the same vehicle restrictions.

Privacy in one line / 隐私一句话

English

Sources, credentials, and guide data stay on-device by default. In version 1.2.1 and later, Google Analytics and Crashlytics measure usage and faults. Usage & fault reporting is on by default and can be turned off. See the Privacy Policy and open-source licenses.
