常见问题Questions

Channo 提供频道或直播源吗？Does Channo include channels or a live-stream list?

不提供。Channo 是 iPhone、iPad 和 Apple TV 上的播放器，只播放你导入的 M3U、M3U8 或 Xtream。它不内置直播源，也不出售频道订阅。No. Channo is a player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. It plays only the M3U, M3U8 or Xtream sources you import. It does not bundle live streams, and it does not sell channel subscriptions.

怎么在 iPhone 上添加 M3U 或 M3U8？How do I add an M3U or M3U8 playlist on iPhone?

在 App Store 下载 Channo，打开来源，选择 M3U 列表，粘贴播放列表地址，或从文件打开 .m3u、.m3u8、.txt。免费版可以添加 3 个播放列表。步骤见 M3U 导入说明。Download Channo from the App Store, open Sources, choose an M3U playlist, paste the playlist address, or open a .m3u, .m3u8 or .txt file. The free version can hold 3 playlists. See the M3U setup guide.

Xtream Codes 要填哪些信息？What do I enter for an Xtream Codes login?

填写服务器地址、用户名和密码。服务器使用 http 或 https。登录后可以查看该账号中的直播、影片和剧集。步骤见 Xtream 登录说明。Enter the server address, username and password. The server uses http or https. After login you can browse that account's live channels, movies and series. See the Xtream login guide.

Apple TV 能使用同一个播放列表吗？Can Apple TV use the same playlist?

可以。在 Apple TV 上安装 Channo 后直接添加链接，或开启 iCloud 同步，让 iPhone 上的来源出现在电视上。步骤见 Apple TV 设置。Yes. Install Channo on Apple TV and add the link there, or turn on iCloud sync so a source from iPhone appears on the TV. See the Apple TV setup.

XMLTV 节目单怎么添加？How do I add an XMLTV guide?

添加来源时填写你自己的 XMLTV 地址，或稍后在来源详情中填写。支持 .xml 和 .gz。免费版查看现在和接下来，Pro 可查看全天节目单。Enter your own XMLTV address when you add a source, or later in the source details. .xml and .gz are supported. Now and Next are free. Pro opens the full-day guide.

Channo 有广告或月费吗？Does Channo show ads or charge a monthly fee?

没有广告，也没有订阅。App 可以免费下载。Pro 是一次购买，用于解锁无限播放列表、全天节目单、全部来源的 iCloud 同步、不限量点播下载和续播。There are no ads and no subscription. The app is free to download. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited playlists, the full-day guide, iCloud sync for every source, unlimited on-demand downloads and resume.
