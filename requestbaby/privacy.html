隐私说明Privacy

requestbaby 是在你自己电脑上运行的工具：你的数据留在你的项目目录里。requestbaby runs on your own computer, and your data stays in your project directory.

更新于 2026 年 11 月 19 日Updated November 19, 2026

没有账号，没有统计No account, no analytics

requestbaby 不需要注册，不包含广告，也不收集使用统计或崩溃数据。requestbaby needs no sign-up, contains no ads and collects no usage statistics or crash data.

数据保存在哪里Where your data lives

用例、环境变量、响应记录、截图、报告和运行历史都保存在你自己的项目目录里。密钥放在不入库的本机文件中。requestbaby 不会把它们上传到任何服务器。Cases, environment variables, recorded responses, screenshots, reports and run history all stay in your own project directory. Secrets live in local files kept out of version control. requestbaby does not upload any of it to a server.

它会连接哪里What it connects to

运行用例时，requestbaby 只连接你在用例里写下的地址（以及你配置的代理）。除此之外，唯一的自动联网是检查新版本：桌面端启动时、或运行 When you run cases, requestbaby connects only to the addresses written in your cases, plus the proxy you configure. The only other automatic network call is the version check: the desktop app at launch, or requestbaby upgrade 时，向 GitHub 查询最新发布。桌面端可以在项目菜单里取消“启动时自动检查更新”，也可以设置环境变量 , asks GitHub for the latest release. You can turn the automatic check off in the desktop app’s project menu, or set REQUESTBABY_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1。.

AI 与模型AI and models

判断通过与否不调用大模型。如果你让自己的 AI 工具读写用例，那是你与该工具之间的事，请按其隐私条款处理，并避免把密钥贴进对话。No model is called to judge results. If you let your own AI tool read or write cases, that is between you and that tool: follow its privacy terms and avoid pasting secrets into a chat.
