requestbaby · Privacy

Privacy

requestbaby runs on your own computer, and your data stays in your project directory.

Updated November 19, 2026

No account, no analytics

requestbaby needs no sign-up, contains no ads and collects no usage statistics or crash data.

Where your data lives

Cases, environment variables, recorded responses, screenshots, reports and run history all stay in your own project directory. Secrets live in local files kept out of version control. requestbaby does not upload any of it to a server.

What it connects to

When you run cases, requestbaby connects only to the addresses written in your cases, plus the proxy you configure. The only other automatic network call is the version check: the desktop app at launch, or requestbaby upgrade, asks GitHub for the latest release. You can turn the automatic check off in the desktop app’s project menu, or set REQUESTBABY_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1.

AI and models

No model is called to judge results. If you let your own AI tool read or write cases, that is between you and that tool: follow its privacy terms and avoid pasting secrets into a chat.

Open source and contact

The source is public under the MIT license on GitHub, so you can check these statements yourself. Questions are welcome in Issues.
