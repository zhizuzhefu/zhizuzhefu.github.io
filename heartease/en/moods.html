Six moods · on device

Pick the feeling you have right now

Everyday, Anxious, Tired, Lost, Self-Doubt, and Bedtime each have their own lines. All 256 were written by hand in Chinese and English and ship inside the app.

How the next line is chosen

HeartEase skips the last 10 lines you saw in the selected mood, and it also skips the line on screen. When every remaining line has been seen, it draws from the mood again and still tries not to repeat the current line at once.

You can save a card or copy the words. For the Home Screen, continue to the widget guide.

Questions

What do I get if I choose Anxious?

You see one bilingual line from the Anxious set. HeartEase does not assess symptoms or offer a diagnosis.

Will the same line repeat immediately?

The next pick avoids the last 10 lines in that mood and the line currently on screen. After the set is exhausted, it may reuse a line while still trying to avoid the current one.
