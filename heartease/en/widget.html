Home Screen · iOS 17

Leave one line where you can see it

Open HeartEase once so it can share the current line with the widget. The words stay on this iPhone.

Add it

  1. Open HeartEase and let the current line sync.
  2. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
  3. Tap the plus button and search for HeartEase.
  4. Choose Today’s Quote or Companion, then add the widget.

Today’s Quote is available in small and medium. Companion, the small cat, is small only. Mood choices are described in moods.

Questions

Does the widget change by itself every day?

It shows the line the app last synced. Changing the line in HeartEase updates the widget. Only when nothing has been synced does it use the day’s fixed Everyday quote.

Which widget sizes are available?

Today’s Quote comes in small and medium. Companion is small.
