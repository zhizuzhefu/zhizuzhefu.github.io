把一句宽心话留在桌面上Leave one line where you can see it

小组件让你不用打开 App，也能看见最近那一句。需要 iOS 17 或更新版本。文案和设置都在这台手机上，不上传。The widget keeps the latest line visible without opening the app. It needs iOS 17 or later. The words stay on this iPhone.

添加步骤Add it

  1. 先打开一次宽心，让它把当前这句话同步给小组件。
  2. 回到主屏幕，长按空白处，直到图标开始抖动。
  3. 点左上角的加号，搜索「宽心」。
  4. 选择「今日宽心」或「宽宽陪伴」，再点添加小组件。
  1. Open HeartEase and let the current line sync.
  2. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
  3. Tap the plus button and search for HeartEase.
  4. Choose Today's Quote or Companion, then add the widget.

两种小组件Two widgets

  • 今日宽心：显示一句文案，有小尺寸和中尺寸。Today's Quote: one line, in small and medium.
  • 宽宽陪伴：小尺寸，是那只陪伴猫和一句提醒。Companion: small only — the cat and a short reminder.

你在 App 里换心情或换一句，小组件会跟着更新。如果这台手机还没有任何同步记录，它才使用按日期算好的「通用」文案，同一天保持这一句。The widget follows the app: switch moods or draw a new line and it updates. Only when nothing has been synced does it use the day's fixed Everyday quote, keeping that line for the whole day.

常见问题Questions

小组件为什么不是每天自动换一句？Does the widget change by itself every day?

它显示 App 最近同步的那一句。你在 App 里换一句，小组件会更新。只有还没有同步记录时，才使用按日期确定的通用文案，同一天不变。It shows the line the app last synced. Changing the line in HeartEase updates the widget. Only when nothing has been synced does it use the day's fixed Everyday quote.

宽心小组件有哪些尺寸？Which widget sizes are available?

「今日宽心」有小尺寸和中尺寸。「宽宽陪伴」是小尺寸。Today's Quote comes in small and medium. Companion is small.
