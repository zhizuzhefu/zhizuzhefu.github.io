当你焦虑、疲惫、迷茫、自我怀疑的时候，不一定需要大道理，而是需要一句能让心稍微松一点的话。打开宽心，选一个你现在的状态，收下一句温柔的话——它不会催你变好，只是先把你接住。When you're anxious, exhausted, or unsure, you don't need a lecture. You need a single warm line to land first. Open HeartEase, pick how you feel, and let a hand-written line meet you exactly where you are — no preaching, no pressure.

陪伴角色Companion

宽宽是一只小小的陪伴猫，会呼吸、会眨眼、会把信轻轻递给你。它不评判、不催促，只是说一句：我在这里。

动效强度可在设置中调到「柔和」或「关闭」，让你按自己的状态决定它有多活跃。

宽宽 ("Kuan Kuan") is a small round cat who breathes, blinks, and quietly hands you a letter. No judgment, no rush — just a small voice saying: I'm here.

Motion intensity is adjustable in Settings — standard, soft, or off, so you can choose how present they should be.

文案原则Tone guide

  • 不说教、不强迫积极
  • 不否定痛苦、不制造羞耻感
  • 不替你做人生判断、不承诺一定会变好
  • 不医疗化表达

每一条文案都经过严格审核，禁止「想开点」「别难过了」「比你惨的人多了」这类话出现。

  • Never preachy, never "stay positive"
  • Never dismisses your pain, never shames you
  • Never makes life decisions for you, never promises it'll get better
  • Never medicalizes your feelings

Every line is reviewed against this guide. Lines like "cheer up" or "others have it worse" are explicitly forbidden.

常见问题Questions

宽心是心理咨询吗？Is HeartEase therapy?

不是。宽心提供日常鼓励和情绪安抚，不替代专业心理咨询、医疗诊断或治疗。如果你正在经历强烈的痛苦，请联系可信任的人，或拨打当地心理危机援助热线。No. HeartEase offers everyday encouragement and gentle comfort. It is not a substitute for professional counseling, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in serious distress, please reach out to someone you trust or call a local crisis line.

文案需要联网吗？Do the lines need a network?

不需要。256 条中英双语文案和设置都保存在这台 iPhone 上。没有账号，没有广告，也没有第三方追踪。No. All 256 bilingual lines and your settings stay on this iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

怎么把一句话放到桌面？How do I put a line on my Home Screen?

添加宽心的桌面小组件。它显示 App 最近同步的那一句；还没有同步记录时，使用当天确定的通用文案。步骤见桌面小组件。Add the HeartEase widget. It shows the line the app last synced; before anything syncs, it uses the day's fixed Everyday line. See the widget guide.
