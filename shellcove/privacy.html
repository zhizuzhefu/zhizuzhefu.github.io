Cloud Agent 1.6：原生 Agent 与模型服务Cloud Agent 1.6: native Agents and model services

Shellcove 提供已配对电脑上的原生 Agent 任务入口。Agent 账号、模型选择和会话存储由原厂客户端管理；你发送的输入、引用和工具内容可能按原厂设置交给对应模型服务商处理。Shellcove 不提供模型订阅，不复制原生凭据。设备可保存草稿、会话缓存与用户下载的文件；从设备移除配对不会删除原厂会话。

Shellcove connects to native Agents on paired computers. Their accounts, model selection and native stores remain managed by the official clients. Submitted inputs, references and tool content may be processed by the selected model provider under its own terms. Local devices can retain drafts, cached conversations and downloaded files. Removing a pairing does not delete native Agent conversations.

摘要Summary

Shellcove 在你的 iPhone / iPad 和已配对的 Mac 上运行，没有账号，也不收集统计。同一 Wi-Fi 或你自己的 VPN 上，内容直接在两台设备之间，以 TLS 加密。Mac 没有公网 IP 时，内置 Tailcat 可能经过加密中继。中继不能读取桌面、按键或窗口文本，但可能看到连接是否发生、时间和流量大小。开发者不会把这些内容存到自己的服务器。除非你主动发邮件，我们看不到你的使用内容。

Shellcove runs on your iPhone or iPad and the paired Mac. There is no account and no analytics. On the same Wi-Fi or a VPN you run, content moves directly between the two devices over TLS. When the Mac has no public IP, built-in Tailcat may use an encrypted relay. The relay cannot read the desktop, keystrokes, or window text, but it may see that a connection exists, plus timing and size. The developer does not store that content. Unless you email us, we do not see what you do in the app.

数据收集Data Collection

开发者不收集你的任务、会话或文件内容。Shellcove 会按你的操作在已配对设备间传输内容，并在本地保存草稿、会话缓存与下载文件；你提交给原生 Agent 的内容可能由你选择的模型服务商按其条款处理。使用本应用无需注册账号或登录，我们不会收集你的姓名、电子邮件、位置信息或任何其他个人身份信息。本应用没有任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK。

The developer does not collect your task, conversation or file content. Shellcove transfers content between paired devices at your request and can store local drafts, conversation caches and downloaded files. Inputs submitted through native Agents may be processed by your selected model provider under its own terms. No account registration or sign-in is required. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

数据处理方式How Data Is Processed

Shellcove 在已配对设备间传输的任务输入、会话、材料和成果，以及按键、鼠标事件、远程桌面、窗口文本和剪贴板都用自签 TLS 证书、证书钉扎和客户端认证保护。同一 Wi-Fi 或你自己的 VPN 上，这些内容不经过外部服务器。需要穿过 NAT 时，内置 Tailcat 的中继只转发已经加密的流量，不能读取上述内容，也不会把它们存成开发者可查看的文件，但可能看到连接元数据。

文本输入由系统键盘或你选择的输入法生成。你可以使用 iOS 听写或第三方输入法，但 Shellcove 只接收输入框里的最终文本并发送给配对 Mac；Shellcove App 本身不请求麦克风权限，也不请求 Speech Recognition 权限。

Task input, conversations, materials and results transferred between paired devices, along with keystrokes, mouse events, Remote Desktop, Window Text and clipboard content, is protected by a self-signed TLS certificate, certificate pinning, and client authentication. On the same Wi-Fi or your own VPN, it does not pass through an external server. When Tailcat must cross NAT, its relay forwards only the already encrypted traffic. The relay cannot read that content and the developer does not keep a copy, but the relay may see connection metadata.

Text input is produced by the system keyboard or the input method you choose. You may use iOS Dictation or a third-party keyboard, but Shellcove receives only the final text in its input field and forwards that text to the paired Mac. The Shellcove app itself does not request Microphone or Speech Recognition permission.

权限说明Permissions

  • 相机：仅用于扫描 Mac 上显示的配对二维码。除了扫描那一刻，相机不会启动。
  • 本地网络：用于与配对的 Mac 建立 TLS 连接。这是 Shellcove 的核心功能必须的权限。

Mac 的设备工具使用你授予的「辅助功能」（键盘、鼠标、文本）和「屏幕录制」（远程桌面捕获）；原生 Agent 的文件与工具授权由其自身管理。Shellcove iOS App 不请求麦克风、语音识别、位置、通讯录、相册、健康、运动、蓝牙、Face ID 或任何其他敏感权限。

  • Camera: only used to scan the pairing QR code shown on your Mac. Outside that moment, the camera is not active.
  • Local Network: required to establish the TLS connection to the paired Mac. Core functionality depends on this.

Mac device tools use the Accessibility (keyboard, mouse and text input) and Screen Recording (Remote Desktop) permissions you grant. Native Agents manage their own file and tool permissions. The Shellcove iOS app does not request Microphone, Speech Recognition, location, contacts, photos, health, motion, Bluetooth, Face ID, or any other sensitive data.

本地存储Local Storage

iPhone / iPad 本地保存配对连接记录、证书指纹和凭据、草稿、会话与原文缓存，以及你下载或导出的文件。常用要求及其名称保存在当前设备，只有你主动发送或导出时才会随操作提供。连接记录使用系统偏好存储；用于绑定本次安装的设备身份保存在 ThisDeviceOnly Keychain 中，不随设备备份克隆到另一部手机。移除某台 Mac 的配对会删除该连接记录，不会删除 Mac 上的原生会话或你已另存的文件。

Mac 本地保存 TLS 身份、已配对手机的凭据核验与撤销记录、任务组织资料、回执、输入队列、原生执行绑定，以及上传材料和有界缓存。新手机可使用独立凭据，在 Mac 上单独撤销后，该身份的连接会关闭，旧凭据会被拒绝；其他手机和已接收任务继续。旧共享配对需要按 Mac 界面的明确提示迁移。原生 Agent 的账号、模型配置和会话历史继续由原生客户端保存与管理。

iPhone / iPad locally stores pairing connection records, certificate fingerprints and credentials, drafts, conversation and original-text caches, and downloaded or exported files. Saved instructions and their names stay on this device and are included only when you choose to send or export them. Connection records use system preferences. The installation identity is held in a ThisDeviceOnly Keychain item and is not cloned to another phone by a device backup. Forgetting a Mac removes its connection record; it does not delete native conversations on the Mac or files you saved separately.

The Mac locally stores its TLS identity, phone credential verification and revocation records, task organization, receipts, input queues, native execution bindings, uploaded materials and bounded caches. New phones can use independent credentials. Revoking a phone on the Mac closes that identity's connections and rejects its old credentials; other phones and accepted work continue. Shared legacy pairings migrate only through the explicit Mac UI flow. Native clients continue to own Agent accounts, model settings and conversation history.

Shellcove is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Since the app collects no user data at all, this poses no data-related risk to any age group.
