Shellcove 1.5 · your own Mac

See the Mac desktop and type into it

Shellcove streams the desktop as video, reads the focused window as real text, and sends the finished string from the iOS keyboard. It is meant for a terminal, a coding agent, or a long task on a machine you already own.

Questions

Do I need a public IP to control my Mac?

No. Same-network Wi-Fi is fastest. Otherwise Automatic mode uses built-in Tailcat, which tries a direct path and then an encrypted relay. Your own VPN still works. A Tailscale account is not required.

Can a relay read my screen?

No. Desktop, window text, clipboard, and keystrokes stay inside the pinned TLS session. A relay forwards ciphertext and may see connection metadata such as timing and size. Shellcove has no account and does not store the picture on the developer’s server.

How do I pair?

Open Shellcove on the Mac and scan the QR code with iPhone or iPad. The Mac needs Accessibility and Screen Recording permission. See how to pair.
