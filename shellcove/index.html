English

Your Mac, live in your hand

Shellcove turns your iPhone or iPad into a local Mac remote. Stream the desktop as video and drive it by touch: move the cursor, pinch to zoom, scroll, and drag. Compose text with the iOS keyboard, iOS Dictation, or any third-party input method, then send it to the focused Mac window. Add a full keyboard, mouse buttons, live Window Text, and clipboard sync for terminal work, AI coding agents, demos, and long-running tasks. Same-network Wi-Fi is fastest. Built-in Tailcat connects without a public IP or a separate Tailscale app. A relay forwards ciphertext only. Your own VPN still works. No account and no analytics.

Live screen / 实时投屏
简体中文
  • 把 Mac 桌面以硬件视频流投到手机（ScreenCaptureKit 采集 + VideoToolbox 编码，iOS 硬件解码，低 CPU / 低功耗）—— 不是逐帧截图
  • 视频本身就是操控面：点按或拖动移动 Mac 光标、捏合放大精确定位、双指滚动、长按拖拽选择文本 / 移动窗口
  • 内置键盘按钮提供鼠标点击（单击 / 右键 / 双击 / 长按）和全部按键；多显示器可任选一块投屏
  • 远程桌面优先走 UDP/DTLS 媒体通道，不可用时回落到 TCP；弱网时优先保留清晰度，降低帧率和码率来恢复画面
English
  • Streams the Mac desktop as a hardware video feed (ScreenCaptureKit capture + VideoToolbox encode → hardware decode on iOS, low CPU/power) — not frame-by-frame screenshots
  • The video is the control surface: tap or drag to move the Mac cursor, pinch to zoom for precision, two-finger scroll, long-press to drag (select text, move windows)
  • A built-in keyboard button adds mouse clicks (Click / Right / Double / Long-press) and every key; pick any monitor to mirror on a multi-display Mac
  • Remote Desktop prefers a UDP/DTLS media channel and falls back to TCP when needed; on weak links it preserves clarity first, then lowers frame rate and bitrate so the picture recovers

  • Compose with the regular keyboard, iOS Dictation, WeChat Keyboard, or any third-party input method. Shellcove receives the final text and types it character by character into the focused Mac window
  • Shellcove does not request Microphone or Speech Recognition permission. iOS Dictation is a system keyboard feature handled before text reaches Shellcove

English

  • "Window Text" opens a live, monospaced view of the focused Mac window's text, such as a terminal buffer. It refreshes as content changes, supports wide-line scrolling, and does not use the video pipeline

Networking / 网络
简体中文
  • 同一 Wi-Fi 最快。Mac 把可达的局域网或你自己的 VPN 地址写入配对二维码。iOS 按完整的主机和端口保存，不会把中继主机和 Mac 的本地端口拼错
  • 没有公网 IP、也不想另装 Tailscale 时，自动模式会启动内置 Tailcat：先尝试打洞，不行再走加密中继。只选直连时不会启动 Tailcat。Tailcat 这一路使用 TCP；局域网直连仍优先走 UDP/DTLS
  • 中继转发的是 TLS 保护后的密文，不能读取桌面、文本或按键，但可能看到连接元数据。设置里仍可手动添加中继的主机和端口；每行显示的是 TCP 连接耗时，不是 ICMP ping
English
  • Same-network Wi-Fi is fastest. The QR code includes reachable LAN or VPN addresses, stored as a host and port so a relay is never joined to the Mac's local port
  • Without a public IP, Automatic mode starts built-in Tailcat: it tries a direct path, then an encrypted relay. Direct-only mode never starts Tailcat. Tailcat uses TCP; LAN video still prefers UDP/DTLS
  • A relay forwards TLS ciphertext and cannot read the desktop, text, or keystrokes, though it may see connection metadata. You can still add a manual relay host and port. Each row shows TCP connect time, not ICMP ping
Privacy / 隐私
简体中文
  • 没有账号，没有广告，没有统计。开发者不会把画面存到自己的服务器
  • TLS + 证书钉扎。Mac 首次启动生成自签证书，设备在配对时把指纹固定下来，只有你配对过的那台 Mac 能完成握手
  • 双向认证。配对二维码还带一枚每台 Mac 独有的 256 位密钥，设备每次连接都要出示；验证通过前 Mac 丢弃它的一切消息 —— 局域网里别的设备摸到端口也注入不了按键
  • 同一 Wi-Fi 或你自己的 VPN 上，流量直接在两台设备之间。穿过 NAT 时，内置 Tailcat 的中继只转发密文，不能读取桌面、窗口文本、剪贴板或按键，但可能看到连接元数据
  • 文本由系统键盘或你选择的输入法生成；Shellcove 只接收最终文本，不请求麦克风或语音识别权限
English
  • No account, no ads, and no analytics. The developer does not store the picture on a server of their own.
  • TLS with certificate pinning. Mac generates a self-signed cert at first launch; your device pins its fingerprint at pairing. Only your paired Mac can complete the TLS handshake.
  • Client authentication. The pairing QR also carries a 256-bit per-install secret the device presents on every connect; until it validates, the Mac drops all of that client's messages — so no other device that reaches the port can inject input.
  • On the same Wi-Fi or your own VPN, traffic stays between the two devices. Across NAT, Tailcat's relay forwards ciphertext only. It cannot read the desktop, window text, clipboard, or keystrokes, but it may see connection metadata.
  • Text is composed by the system keyboard or input method you choose. Shellcove receives only the final text and does not request Microphone or Speech Recognition permission.

常见问题

用 iPhone 控制 Mac 需要公网 IP 吗？

不需要。同一 Wi-Fi 最快。不在同一网络时，内置 Tailcat 会尝试打洞，不行再走加密中继。中继只转发密文。你自己的 VPN 也可以。不需要另装 Tailscale，也不需要账号。

Shellcove 会把桌面存到服务器吗？

不会存到开发者的服务器，也没有账号和统计。直连时流量在 iPhone 或 iPad 与已配对的 Mac 之间。使用加密中继时，中继不能读取桌面、窗口文本、剪贴板或按键，但可能看到连接元数据。

怎么和 Mac 配对？

在 Mac 上打开 Shellcove，用 iPhone 或 iPad 扫描配对二维码。Mac 需要辅助功能和屏幕录制权限。步骤见如何配对。
