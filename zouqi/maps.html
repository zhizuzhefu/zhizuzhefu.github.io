步行 · 按攻略顺序Walking · in guide order

读完攻略，再把地点交给地图One stop or the whole list

走起自己不画导航线。它把已经有坐标的地点，按文章里的顺序交给地图 App。高德使用国测局坐标，百度使用百度坐标。主题见 北京周末去哪。ZouQi draws no route lines of its own. It hands geocoded places, in article order, to a map app. Amap uses GCJ-02 coordinates and Baidu uses BD-09. Topics are in the Beijing guide.

一个地点，或一整条One place, or the whole route

  • 一个地点：Apple 地图、高德或百度，都按步行打开。One place: Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps, all opened as walking directions.
  • 两个或更多地点：只出现高德和百度。当前位置是起点，其余地点按顺序作为途经点，最后一个是终点。Two or more places: only Amap and Baidu Maps appear. The current position is the start, the rest become ordered stops, the last one the destination.
  • 没有坐标的地点会被留下，不参与这次导航。Places without coordinates are left out of that navigation.
  • 高德和百度需要另外安装。只有单点失败时才会打开网页；多点路线失败就停住，不降级成只剩终点。Amap and Baidu Maps install separately. Only a single stop falls back to the web; a failed multi-stop route stops instead of quietly dropping stops.

常见问题Questions

为什么整条路线不能交给 Apple 地图？Why can't the whole route go to Apple Maps?

多个地点时，走起只把完整顺序交给高德或百度，起点用地图里的当前位置，方式是步行。Apple 地图只接收一个有坐标的地点，同样按步行打开。For several places, ZouQi hands the full order to Amap or Baidu Maps with the current position as the start, walking. Apple Maps receives a single geocoded place, also on foot.

没装高德或百度会怎样？What if Amap or Baidu Maps isn't installed?

只有一个地点时，可以退回对应的网页地图。两个以上地点时不会改成网页，以免途经点被悄悄丢掉。没有坐标的地点一开始就不会进入导航。With one place, the app can fall back to that map's website. With two or more, it does not downgrade to the web, so stops are never silently dropped. Places without coordinates never enter navigation.
