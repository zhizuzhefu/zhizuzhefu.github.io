常见问题与使用帮助 / FAQ & Help

Discovering guides / 发现攻略

简体中文
  • 在首页品牌名称下方选择 App 当前已开放的城市。
  • 「推荐」会结合所选城市的天气上下文、内容新鲜度等规则；天气不可用时按新鲜度降级。
  • 「最新」按更新时间浏览。点攻略卡片可查看正文、图片和有序地点清单。
English
  • Choose from the currently available cities below the brand name on Home.
  • Recommended considers the selected city's weather context and content freshness; if weather is unavailable, it falls back to freshness.
  • Newest browses by update time. Tap a guide card for the article, media, and ordered place list.

Favorites and sharing / 收藏与分享

简体中文
  • 在攻略详情顶部工具栏点收藏；首页右上角心形入口可查看全部收藏。
  • 收藏只保存在当前设备。已撤下攻略会从收藏中自动清理。
  • 分享会生成稳定公开链接，可复制链接或使用 iOS 系统分享面板。
English
  • Save a guide from the top toolbar; open all favorites from the heart button at the top right of Home.
  • Favorites stay on this device. Withdrawn guides are removed automatically.
  • Sharing uses a stable public link that you can copy or send through the iOS share sheet.

Navigation and map apps / 导航与地图 App

简体中文
  • 单点导航：地点具备真实地址和坐标时，可选择 Apple 地图、高德地图或百度地图。
  • 整条路线：攻略至少包含两个有坐标的地点时，从当前位置出发，将全部地点按顺序交给高德地图或百度地图。
  • 未安装高德 / 百度：单点导航可继续使用 Apple 地图；整条多点路线不会静默回退为缺少途经点的 Apple 地图路线，而会提示你安装并使用高德或百度地图。
  • 点位错误：请在攻略详情点「需要改进 / 反馈」，选择「错误」并补充说明。
English
  • Single place: when a place has a verified address and coordinate, open it in Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps.
  • Full route: when a guide has at least two geocoded places, start from the current position and hand every ordered stop to Amap or Baidu Maps.
  • Amap / Baidu not installed: Apple Maps remains available for a single destination. A complete multi-stop route never falls back to an incomplete Apple Maps route; ZouQi asks you to install and use Amap or Baidu Maps instead.
  • Wrong pin: tap Needs Work / Feedback on the guide, choose Wrong, and add details.

Feedback and corrections / 反馈与纠错

简体中文

每篇攻略底部都有「有用」和「需要改进」。需要改进时可选原因，并选填最多 300 字的备注和一张系统照片选择器选中的照片。提交失败时，表单会保留输入，方便重试。

反馈不会直接公开，只进入运营人员可见的私有队列。运营人员审核后可以纠错、补充或撤下攻略。你也可以使用本站反馈页面通过邮件联系我们。

English

Every guide offers Helpful and Needs Work actions. Needs Work can include a reason, an optional note of up to 300 characters, and one photo selected through the system Photos picker. If submission fails, the form keeps your input for retry.

Feedback is never published directly. It enters a private operator queue for review, correction, additions, or withdrawal. You can also contact us by email from the website Feedback page.

Language and App menu / 语言与应用菜单

简体中文

在首页右上角点击三横线「应用菜单」，可选择跟随系统、简体中文或 English，也可以打开隐私政策、用户协议、帮助与支持和意见反馈。

English

Tap the three-line App menu at the top right of Home to follow the system language, choose Simplified Chinese or English, or open Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Help & Support, and Feedback.

Network and loading problems / 网络与加载问题

简体中文
  • 攻略内容来自在线服务，没有内置业务数据或离线内容兜底。
  • 加载失败时先确认网络可用，再点「重试」或下拉刷新。
  • 如果某张图片或某个地点缺失，可能是内容已撤下或正在维护；可通过反馈入口告诉我们具体攻略名称。
English
  • Guide content comes from the online service; there is no bundled business data or offline fallback.
  • If loading fails, confirm your connection, then tap Retry or pull to refresh.
  • If media or a place is missing, it may have been withdrawn or be under maintenance. Send the guide name through Feedback.

Privacy and account questions / 隐私与账号

简体中文

走起没有账号系统，不请求定位、相机或完整照片图库权限，也没有广告、分析或追踪 SDK。删除本地数据只需卸载 App；如需查询或删除已提交的攻略反馈，请按隐私政策中的方式联系我们。

English

ZouQi has no account system, requests no location, camera, or full photo-library permission, and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. Uninstall the app to remove local data; to ask about or delete submitted guide feedback, follow the contact process in the Privacy Policy.

English

Still need help, need to report objectionable content, or want to correct a guide? Email zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com. Where relevant, include the guide name, issue, device model, iOS version, and app version. Never send passwords or other sensitive information.
