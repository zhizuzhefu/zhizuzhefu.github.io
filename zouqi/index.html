好攻略，拿起就能走

从发现页挑一篇适合当下的城市攻略，读完重点，再按顺序查看地点。喜欢的内容可以收藏或分享；准备出发时，把单个地点或整条路线交给 Apple 地图、高德地图或百度地图。

即将上线 App Store · Coming soon iOS 17+ · 简体中文 / English
English

Good guides, ready to go.

ZouQi is a guide-first city outings app. Discover editor-curated articles, follow each guide's ordered place list, save or share what you like, then hand one destination or the full route to Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps when it is time to leave.

Discover / 发现
简体中文
  • 选择 App 已开放的城市，查看对应攻略内容
  • 在「推荐」与「最新」之间切换；推荐会结合城市天气上下文与内容新鲜度
  • 双列攻略卡片清楚显示标题、封面和地点数量
English
  • Choose from the cities currently available in the app
  • Switch between Recommended and Newest; recommendations consider city weather context and freshness
  • Two-column guide cards keep titles, covers, and place counts easy to scan
Read & save / 阅读与收藏
简体中文
  • 攻略详情包含图片、正文、更新时间和有序地点清单
  • 地点详情提供说明、地址和关联攻略
  • 收藏保存在设备本地；攻略撤下后会从收藏中自动清理
English
  • Guide details include media, article text, update time, and an ordered place list
  • Place details include a description, address, and related guides
  • Favorites stay on your device and withdrawn guides are removed automatically
Navigate & share / 导航与分享
简体中文
  • 有真实坐标的地点可交给 Apple 地图、高德地图或百度地图导航
  • 包含至少两个可导航地点的攻略支持整条路线接力
  • 通过稳定公开链接复制或使用 iOS 系统分享
English
  • Open geocoded places in Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps
  • Guides with at least two navigable places can hand off the full route
  • Copy a stable public link or use the iOS share sheet
Feedback / 纠错与补充
简体中文
  • 可标记攻略有用或需要改进，并选填原因、文字和一张照片
  • 反馈只进入运营人员可见的私有队列，不会直接公开
  • 所有公开攻略均由运营人员另行审核、编辑并发布
English
  • Mark a guide helpful or needing work, with an optional reason, note, and one photo
  • Feedback enters a private operator queue and is never published directly
  • Every public guide is separately reviewed, edited, and published by the operator
Privacy / 隐私
简体中文
  • 无需账号，不含广告、第三方分析 SDK 或跨 App 追踪
  • 不请求定位、相机或完整照片图库权限
  • App 内容来自开发者自有服务；断网时显示错误和重试，不使用内置业务数据兜底
English
  • No account, ads, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking
  • No location, camera, or full photo-library permission
  • Content comes from the developer-owned service; offline errors show retry instead of bundled business content
Requirements / 系统要求

English
  • iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or newer
  • Simplified Chinese and English, switchable from the top-right home menu
  • Network access is required for guides; Amap or Baidu Maps must be installed separately
