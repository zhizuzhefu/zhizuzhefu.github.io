发现Discover

  • 选择 App 已开放的城市，查看对应攻略内容
  • 在「推荐」与「最新」之间切换；推荐会结合城市天气上下文与内容新鲜度
  • 双列攻略卡片清楚显示标题、封面和地点数量
  • Choose from the cities currently available in the app
  • Switch between Recommended and Newest; recommendations consider city weather context and freshness
  • Two-column guide cards keep titles, covers, and place counts easy to scan

阅读与收藏Read & save

  • 攻略详情包含图片、正文、更新时间和有序地点清单
  • 地点详情提供说明、地址和关联攻略
  • 收藏保存在设备本地；攻略撤下后会从收藏中自动清理
  • Guide details include media, article text, update time, and an ordered place list
  • Place details include a description, address, and related guides
  • Favorites stay on your device and withdrawn guides are removed automatically

导航与分享Navigate & share

  • 有真实坐标的单个地点可交给 Apple 地图、高德地图或百度地图导航
  • 包含至少两个可导航地点的攻略可从当前位置出发，将全部地点按顺序交给高德或百度地图
  • 通过稳定公开链接复制或使用 iOS 系统分享
  • Open one geocoded place in Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps
  • Start from the current position and preserve every ordered stop in an Amap or Baidu multi-stop route
  • Copy a stable public link or use the iOS share sheet

纠错与补充Feedback

  • 可标记攻略有用或需要改进，并选填原因、文字和一张照片
  • 反馈只进入运营人员可见的私有队列，不会直接公开
  • 所有公开攻略均由运营人员另行审核、编辑并发布
  • Mark a guide helpful or needing work, with an optional reason, note, and one photo
  • Feedback enters a private operator queue and is never published directly
  • Every public guide is separately reviewed, edited, and published by the operator

常见问题Questions

走起会自动生成行程吗？Does ZouQi invent an itinerary for me?

不会。走起展示编辑整理并发布的攻略。每篇有正文、图片和按顺序排列的地点，它不替你生成行程。No. ZouQi shows guides that an editor reviews and publishes. Each one has an article, images, and places in order.

北京周末现在有哪些主题？Which Beijing topics are available now?

当前内容包括清华北大校园、清真美食、驻京办与使馆区、博物馆和图书馆、花市故居公园、亲子工业旅游，以及北京出发的高铁周边游。App 里的城市列表以线上目录为准。Current guides include the Tsinghua and Peking University campuses, halal food, liaison-office and embassy-area dining, museums and libraries, flower markets, former homes and parks, family industrial visits, and rail day trips from Beijing. The city list in the app comes from the live catalog.

怎么把攻略交给地图？How do I open a guide in a map?

单个有坐标的地点可以交给 Apple 地图、高德或百度，按步行导航。至少两个地点时，整条路线交给高德或百度，起点是当前位置。没有坐标的地点不会放进导航。步骤见交给地图。One geocoded place opens in Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps as walking directions. Two or more places open as a full walking route in Amap or Baidu Maps, starting from the current location. Places without coordinates are left out. See maps.
