Questions

Does ZouQi invent an itinerary for me?

No. ZouQi shows guides that an editor reviews and publishes. Each one has an article, images, and places in order.

Which Beijing topics are available now?

Current guides include the Tsinghua and Peking University campuses, halal food, liaison-office and embassy-area dining, museums and libraries, flower markets, former homes and parks, family industrial visits, and rail day trips from Beijing. The city list in the app comes from the live catalog.

How do I open a guide in a map?

One geocoded place opens in Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps as walking directions. Two or more places open as a full walking route in Amap or Baidu Maps, starting from the current location. Places without coordinates are left out. See maps.
