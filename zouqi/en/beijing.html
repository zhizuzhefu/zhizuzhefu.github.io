Questions

Are these Beijing guides generated automatically?

No. A public guide is reviewed and published by an editor. You can mark one helpful or in need of a fix. Feedback goes to a private queue and is not posted automatically.

Can I read a guide with no network?

No. If the network fails, ZouQi shows an error and a retry instead of substituting bundled articles. Favorites stay on the device, and a withdrawn guide is removed from them.
