Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

走起 ZouQi 隐私政策

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-07-31

Summary / 摘要

简体中文

走起无需注册或登录，不含广告、第三方分析 SDK，也不进行跨 App 追踪。App 通过开发者自有服务读取城市、天气上下文、攻略、地点和图片。只有当你主动提交攻略反馈时，才会发送你选择提供的反馈内容；这些内容不绑定账号，仅用于改进攻略。

English

ZouQi requires no registration or sign-in, contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track users across apps. The app reads cities, weather context, guides, places, and media from the developer-owned service. Only when you explicitly submit guide feedback does it send the content you choose to provide; this content is not linked to an account and is used only to improve guides.

Data we process / 我们处理的数据

简体中文
  • 内容读取：你选择的城市以及读取攻略、地点、图片和天气上下文所需的请求。请求不包含账号身份、设备定位、收藏或语言偏好。
  • 可选攻略反馈：攻略标识、有用 / 需要改进、选填原因、最多 300 字的选填备注，以及你明确选择的一张照片。
  • 网站邮件反馈：本站反馈表单只在你的设备上生成邮件草稿，不直接提交到网站服务器。只有你在邮件 App 中确认发送后，我们才会收到你填写的内容，以及你自愿提供的联系方式、设备和 App 版本信息。
  • 随机幂等标识：App 为每篇已反馈攻略生成一个随机标识并随反馈发送，用于把重复反馈更新为同一条记录。它不是广告标识，不用于识别真实身份或追踪。
  • 必要网络信息：与任何互联网服务一样，托管与安全基础设施可能在传输和防滥用过程中处理 IP 地址、请求时间等网络信息。走起不会把这些信息写入反馈记录，也不用于画像、广告或追踪。
English
  • Content requests: the selected city and requests needed to retrieve guides, places, media, and weather context. Requests contain no account identity, device location, favorites, or language preference.
  • Optional guide feedback: the guide identifier, helpful / needs work choice, optional reason, an optional note of up to 300 characters, and one photo you explicitly select.
  • Website email feedback: the website form creates an email draft locally on your device and does not submit directly to a website server. We receive the content, and any contact, device, or app-version details you voluntarily include, only after you choose Send in your mail app.
  • Random idempotency identifier: the app generates a random value for each guide you review and sends it with feedback so repeat submissions update the same record. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used to identify or track you.
  • Necessary network data: as with any internet service, hosting and security infrastructure may process an IP address, request time, and similar network information for transmission and abuse prevention. ZouQi does not add this information to feedback records or use it for profiling, advertising, or tracking.

Photos and feedback / 照片与攻略反馈

简体中文

走起不请求相机或完整照片图库权限。只有你在反馈表单中主动点击添加照片、通过 Apple 系统照片选择器选中一项并提交后，App 才会上传这一张照片。已完成登记但没有被反馈引用的上传会由服务端定期清理。

反馈进入仅运营人员可见的私有处理队列，不会自动显示给其他用户。运营人员可以据此纠错；任何后来公开的攻略都需要另行审核、编辑并发布。

English

ZouQi does not request camera or full photo-library access. An image is uploaded only after you tap to add one in the feedback form, select a single item with Apple's system Photos picker, and submit. Uploads that were registered successfully but never referenced by feedback are periodically removed by the service.

Feedback enters a private queue visible only to operators and is never shown automatically to other users. Operators may use it to correct a guide; anything later made public must be separately reviewed, edited, and published.

Local storage / 本地存储

简体中文

走起在设备的 UserDefaults 中保存收藏的攻略、所选城市、语言偏好、已提交反馈的选中状态和随机幂等标识。这些本地数据不通过走起同步到云端；删除 App 会清除这些本地数据。攻略被撤下后，对应收藏会自动清理。

English

ZouQi stores favorites, selected city, language preference, submitted-feedback state, and random idempotency identifiers in UserDefaults on the device. ZouQi does not sync this local data to the cloud; deleting the app removes it. Favorites for withdrawn guides are also cleaned up automatically.

No location collection / 不收集设备定位

简体中文

走起不请求设备定位权限，也不会读取或上传你的精确或粗略位置。推荐中使用的天气信息属于你在 App 内所选城市的公共天气上下文，不是设备位置。

English

ZouQi does not request location permission and does not read or upload your precise or coarse device location. Weather used for recommendations is public context for the city selected in the app, not device location.

Maps, sharing, and service providers / 地图、分享与服务提供商

简体中文
  • 点击导航后，走起会把单个地点交给你选择的 Apple 地图、高德地图或百度地图，或把完整多点路线交给高德地图或百度地图；之后适用对应地图 App 的隐私政策。
  • 使用系统分享时，公开攻略链接只会交给你在 iOS 分享面板中选择的服务。
  • 开发者自有 API、数据库和图片存储运行在 Cloudflare 基础设施上；公开产品网页托管在 GitHub Pages。这些服务商为托管、安全和传输处理必要数据。
  • 我们要求代表我们处理数据的服务商遵守适用的数据保护义务，仅为提供约定服务处理数据，并提供不低于本政策所述的保护。
  • 我们不出售个人数据，不向广告网络或数据经纪商提供数据。
English
  • When you navigate, ZouQi hands one destination to Apple Maps, Amap, or Baidu Maps, or a complete multi-stop route to Amap or Baidu Maps; that maps app's privacy policy then applies.
  • When you use the system share sheet, the public guide link goes only to the service you choose in iOS.
  • The developer-owned API, database, and media storage run on Cloudflare infrastructure; the public product pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process necessary data for hosting, security, and transmission.
  • We require service providers processing data on our behalf to follow applicable data-protection obligations, process data only for the agreed service, and provide protection no less protective than described in this policy.
  • We do not sell personal data or provide it to advertising networks or data brokers.

Retention, deletion, and choices / 保留、删除与选择

简体中文

App 内可选反馈、网站邮件反馈和所附照片仅在处理纠错、提供支持、维护攻略质量和防止重复提交所需期间保留；当不再需要时会删除或去标识化，但法律、安全或争议处理要求继续保留的除外。你可以不提交选填原因、备注、照片或联系方式，也可以随时删除 App 以清除本地偏好。

如需查询或删除已提交的反馈，请发送邮件至 zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com，并尽量提供攻略名称、提交的大致日期和反馈内容，便于在没有账号的情况下定位记录。经核实后，我们会删除仍可定位的反馈及其照片。走起没有账号系统，因此不涉及账号删除。

English

Optional in-app feedback, website email feedback, and attached photos are retained only as long as needed to process corrections, provide support, maintain guide quality, and prevent duplicate submissions. They are deleted or de-identified when no longer needed, except where law, security, or dispute handling requires continued retention. You may omit the optional reason, note, photo, or contact details, and may delete the app at any time to remove local preferences.

To ask about or delete submitted feedback, email zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com with the guide name, approximate submission date, and feedback details where possible, so we can locate it without an account. After verification, we will delete feedback and its photo that can still be identified. ZouQi has no account system, so there is no account to delete.

Children's privacy / 儿童隐私

简体中文

走起不是专为儿童设计的服务，也不会有意要求儿童提供个人信息。如果我们发现反馈中包含儿童的个人信息，会在收到通知后删除。监护人可以通过下方联系方式提出请求。

English

ZouQi is not a service specifically directed to children and does not knowingly ask children for personal information. If we learn that feedback contains a child's personal information, we will delete it after notice. A parent or guardian may contact us below.

Changes and contact / 政策变更与联系

简体中文

如果数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页的生效日期，并在适当情况下通过版本说明告知。隐私问题、数据查询或删除请求请发邮件至 zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com。

English

If our data practices change, we update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, explain material changes in release notes. For privacy questions, data inquiries, or deletion requests, email zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com.
