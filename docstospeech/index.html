一个主题，一个工作区One topic, one workspace

多份资料放在一起，原文件、整理后的文字和音频分开查看。可以校对、改名、导出或移除。Keep several sources together. Originals, edited text and audio stay separate. Rename, export or remove them when you want.

先试听，再决定保存Listen before you save

文字转语音有「试听」和「生成并保存」。试听只播放。保存后的文件留在本机，重启后仍可播放。Text to audio has Listen and Create and save. Listen only plays. A saved file stays on the device and still plays after a restart.

尚未开放Not in this version

自建云端讲解、业务账号、额度结算、资料同步和音视频转写尚未开放。当前版本做的是本地整理、识字、朗读和复听。已经打开 Apple 智能、并且本机模型可用的 iOS 26 iPhone，可以把选中原文整理成讲稿再听；没有该模型时仍朗读原文。DocsToSpeech 不把资料上传到自己的服务器。播放完成不代表已经掌握内容。模型随 App 内置，安装体积较大。

Our own cloud explanations, business accounts, credit billing, document sync and audio or video transcription are not enabled. This version organizes, recognizes, speaks and replays material on the device. An iPhone on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence and the on-device model ready can turn selected text into a spoken explanation; otherwise it still reads the original text. DocsToSpeech does not upload that material to its own servers. Finishing playback does not prove that the material is mastered. Models are bundled, so the download is large.

常见问题Questions

怎么在 iPhone 上听自己的 PDF？How do I listen to my own PDF on iPhone?

建立一个工作区并导入 PDF。核对提取出的文字；扫描页会走本地识字，必要时改一下。然后试听，或生成并保存，再到音频库播放。步骤见 支持。Create a workspace and import the PDF. Check the extracted text. Scanned pages use on-device recognition, and you can correct them. Then listen, or create and save, and play the file from the audio library. Steps are on the support page.

需要单独安装音色包吗？Do I install a voice pack separately?

不需要。中英文男声和女声已经内置。你也可以改用系统里已经安装的声音。No. Chinese and English male and female voices are already in the app. You can also use a system voice that is installed on the device.

再次播放还要联网或重新合成吗？Does replay need a network or another synthesis?

已保存的音频从本机播放，不会再次合成。需要另存一份时，请自己导出。Saved audio plays from the device. It is not synthesized again. Export a copy yourself when you want one outside the app.

资料会上传吗？Are documents uploaded?

不会自动上传。文档、识别结果和音频保存在这台设备上。详情见 隐私政策。They are not uploaded automatically. Documents, recognition results and audio stay on this device. See the privacy policy.
