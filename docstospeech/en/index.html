One topic, one workspace

Keep several sources together. Originals, edited text and audio stay separate. Rename, export or remove them when you want.

Listen before you save

Text to audio has Listen and Create and save. Listen only plays. A saved file stays on the device and still plays after a restart.

Text from a page or a screenshot

Recognition runs on the device. After a screenshot, photo, capture or scan, compare the text with the original before you add it to a workspace.

Shortcuts

Tools includes an offline guide. The Shortcuts app can run Recognize text offline and Create offline audio.

What this version does

  • Import your own PDFs, EPUBs, text, Markdown, photos and scans
  • Bundled CosyVoice Chinese and English male and female voices; no separate voice pack
  • Or choose a system voice already installed on the device
  • The audio library plays saved local files that passed their checks
  • Sign in with Apple is optional local identity and does not create a cloud account
  • No ads and no analytics SDK. Materials are not uploaded automatically

Not in this version

Our own cloud explanations, business accounts, credit billing, document sync and audio or video transcription are not enabled. This version organizes, recognizes, speaks and replays material on the device. An iPhone on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence and the on-device model ready can turn selected text into a spoken explanation; otherwise it still reads the original text. DocsToSpeech does not upload that material to its own servers. Finishing playback does not prove that the material is mastered. Models are bundled, so the download is large.

Questions

How do I listen to my own PDF on iPhone?

Create a workspace and import the PDF. Check the extracted text. Scanned pages use on-device recognition, and you can correct them. Then listen, or create and save, and play the file from the audio library. Steps are on the support page.

Do I install a voice pack separately?

No. Chinese and English male and female voices are already in the app. You can also use a system voice that is installed on the device.

Does replay need a network or another synthesis?

Saved audio plays from the device. It is not synthesized again. Export a copy yourself when you want one outside the app.

Are documents uploaded?

They are not uploaded automatically. Documents, recognition results and audio stay on this device. See the privacy policy.
