Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

DocsToSpeech Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-31

Summary / 摘要

简体中文

DocsToSpeech 在你的 iPhone 或 iPad 上整理和朗读你自己的资料。文档、照片、识别文字、音频和播放进度保存在本机。内置识字和语音模型在本地处理，不会把这些内容自动上传。没有广告 SDK，也没有分析 SDK。Apple 登录是可选的本机身份。

English

DocsToSpeech organizes and reads your own material on your iPhone or iPad. Documents, photos, recognized text, audio and playback progress stay on the device. Bundled recognition and speech models process those inputs locally and do not upload them automatically. There is no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. Sign in with Apple is an optional identity stored on the device.

On this device / 本机保存什么

简体中文
  • 你导入的原文件、整理后的文字、工作区和播放进度
  • 本地识字结果，以及你校对后的文字
  • 你选择「生成并保存」后的音频。试听文件只在临时目录播放，不写入资料库
  • 若语音未能完成，App 会把提示原文和错误类型记在本机，便于你反馈
  • 你主动导出或分享时，文件才会离开这台设备
English
  • Original files you import, edited text, workspaces and playback progress
  • Local recognition results and the corrections you make
  • Audio you choose to create and save. Listen-only audio plays from a temporary file and is not added to your library
  • If speech cannot finish, the app keeps the shown message and error type on the device so you can report it
  • A file leaves the device only when you export or share it

Apple account / 可选的 Apple 登录

简体中文

登录不是使用本地功能的前提。如果你使用「通过 Apple 登录」，Apple 提供的用户标识，以及你授权分享的姓名或邮箱，会保存在这台设备的钥匙串里。当前版本不会据此创建业务云端账号，也不会同步文档。你可以在「设置 → Apple 账户」里移除本机身份。Apple 侧的授权请在 Apple 账户设置中另行管理。

English

Local features do not require signing in. If you use Sign in with Apple, the user identifier and any name or email you choose to share are stored in this device’s Keychain. This version does not create a business cloud account from that identity and does not sync documents. You can remove the saved identity in Settings → Apple account. Manage Apple’s own authorization in your Apple Account settings.

Camera, files and Shortcuts / 相机、文件与快捷指令

简体中文

相机只在你拍照或扫描时使用，用来把纸张变成可校对的文字。从文件或相册选择资料，也只处理你点选的内容。快捷指令可以把图片、PDF 或文字交给同一套本地工具，并取回识别文字或保存的音频。这些操作会打开 App 完成本地准备。

English

The camera is used when you photograph or scan a page, so the text can be checked. Files and photos are processed only when you choose them. Shortcuts can pass an image, PDF or text to the same local tools and receive recognized text or saved audio. Those actions open the app to finish the local preparation.

What this version does not do / 当前版本不会做的事

简体中文

没有广告，没有分析或追踪 SDK，不会出售你的资料。自建云端讲解、业务账号、额度结算和资料同步尚未开放。在支持的 iPhone 上，你点「用本机模型整理」时，选中文字由系统本机模型处理，DocsToSpeech 不把这段文字上传到自己的服务器。将来如果提供需要上传的处理，会在你确认前说明范围。卸载 App 或删除工作区会移除对应的本机数据，删除后不能从我们的服务器恢复，因为这些内容本来就不在服务器上。

English

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDK, and we do not sell your material. Our own cloud explanations, business accounts, credit billing and document sync are not enabled. When you choose Explain on this device on a supported iPhone, the selected text is processed by the system on-device model. DocsToSpeech does not upload that text to its own servers. If a future feature needs an upload, its scope will be shown before you confirm. Uninstalling the app or deleting a workspace removes that local data. It cannot be restored from our servers, because it was not stored there.

Contact / 联系

简体中文

隐私问题请写到 zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com。请不要在邮件里附上敏感文档。你发给我们的邮件由我们用来回复问题；如果你要求删除某封已发送的邮件内容，请来信说明。

English

Privacy questions: zhizuzhefu@foxmail.com. Do not attach sensitive documents. Mail you send is used to answer you. If you want the contents of a message you already sent deleted, write and say so.
