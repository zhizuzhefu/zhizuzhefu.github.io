Make an iPhone ringtone from your audio

AudioTools cuts a clip from audio you already have. It does not include a song library. Use only recordings you have the right to use.

Steps

  1. Import a song or recording, or first extract audio with video to MP3.
  2. Choose the start point. The ringtone begins there and is no longer than 30 seconds.
  3. Export the .m4r file. The app then shows a GarageBand guide for setting it as the call ringtone.
  4. The free version allows one ringtone export a month. Pro is a one-time purchase and removes that limit.

Questions

How long can an iPhone ringtone be?

AudioTools trims from the start point you choose and keeps at most 30 seconds, then exports an .m4r file.

How many free ringtones can I export?

The free version allows one ringtone a month. Pro removes that monthly limit. Follow the GarageBand guide inside the app to install it.
