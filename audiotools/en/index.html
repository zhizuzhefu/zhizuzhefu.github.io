Video to MP3 and ringtones for iPhone

Extract audio from a video, convert formats, trim, change speed or volume, and export a ringtone. Processing stays on the iPhone or iPad. There is no account and no ad.

What you can do

  • Turn MP4, MOV, AVI, or MKV into MP3, M4A, AAC, Ogg + Opus, or MP4 audio
  • Trim, set speed from 0.2x to 2x, and adjust volume before export
  • Make a ringtone of at most 30 seconds as an .m4r file
  • Import from Files, or from a browser on the same Wi-Fi. The transfer only moves the file onto the phone

Video to MP3 steps · Ringtone steps

Free version and Pro

The free version allows 3 full conversions a day, up to 192 kbps, one file at a time, kept for 7 days, and one ringtone a month. WAV and FLAC are part of Pro.

Pro is a one-time $1.99 purchase: unlimited conversions, up to 320 kbps, WAV and FLAC, unlimited ringtones, and batch conversion. It is not a subscription.

Questions

Is AudioTools completely free?

The app can be downloaded free. There are no ads and no subscription. The free version allows 3 full conversions a day, up to 192 kbps, one file at a time, kept for 7 days, and one ringtone a month. Pro is a one-time $1.99 purchase.

Does video to MP3 upload my file?

No. AudioTools extracts the audio and exports MP3 on the iPhone or iPad. Files are not uploaded, and there is no account.

How do I make an iPhone ringtone?

Trim from a start point, up to 30 seconds, and export an .m4r file. The app then shows a GarageBand setup guide. The free version allows one ringtone a month.
