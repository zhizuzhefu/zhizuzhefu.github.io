音频随转隐私政策AudioTools Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026-10-26Effective date: 2026-10-26

数据收集Data Collection

音频随转在本机处理音频，文件不会上传。无需注册或登录，我们不收集姓名、电子邮件、位置或其他身份信息。新版使用 Google Analytics for Firebase 与 Firebase Crashlytics 统计匿名的使用情况与崩溃：页面访问、导入的文件格式、转换是否成功及失败原因类别、用时与大小区间、Pro 额度提示等。「设置 → 隐私 → 帮助改进 App」默认开启，可随时关闭，统一控制这两项服务；关闭后停止后续采集并重置本地统计数据。我们不向 Google 传入文件名、路径、音频内容或任意错误文本，不使用广告 ID、IDFV 或跨 App 追踪，广告存储与个性化许可保持拒绝。Google 通过应用安装实例标识关联事件，并接收应用/系统版本、设备类型、语言等技术信息，可从掩码 IP 派生大致地区。数据用于改进 App，不用于广告，可能在你所在地区以外处理，受 Google 数据处理条款约束。

AudioTools processes audio on this device; files are never uploaded. No account or sign-in is required, and we do not collect your name, email, location or other identifying information. New builds use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to measure anonymous usage and crashes: screens visited, imported file formats, whether conversions succeed and the category of failure, duration and size ranges, and Pro limit prompts. Settings → Privacy → Help Improve the App is on by default and can be turned off at any time; one setting controls both services, and turning it off stops further collection and resets local analytics data. We never send file names, paths, audio content or raw error text to Google, and we do not use the advertising ID, IDFV or cross-app tracking; ad storage and personalization consent stay denied. Google links events with an app-instance identifier, receives technical information such as app/OS version, device type and language, and may derive an approximate region from a truncated IP address. Data is used to improve the app, not for ads, and may be processed outside your country under Google's data processing terms.

文件处理方式How Files Are Processed

您导入音频随转的所有音频和视频文件均在设备本地完成处理，包括导入、格式转换、音频提取、裁剪、铃声制作及导出操作。所有文件数据始终保留在您的设备上，不会上传至任何外部服务器或云端服务。

All audio and video files you import into AudioTools are processed entirely on your device, including import, format conversion, audio extraction, trimming, ringtone making, and export. Your file data remains on your device at all times and is never uploaded to any external server or cloud service.

权限说明Permissions

  • 文件访问：用于导入您选择的音频或视频文件，并将转换结果保存至本地存储。本应用仅访问您明确选择的文件。
  • 本地网络：仅用于 WiFi 传输功能，允许您在同一局域网内通过浏览器向设备传输文件。此功能不涉及互联网连接或云端通信。
  • 通知（可选）：用于在音频转换完成或出现异常时发送本地设备通知。此权限为可选项，拒绝授权不影响任何核心功能的使用。
  • File Access: Required to import audio or video files you select and to save conversion results to local storage. The app only accesses files you explicitly choose.
  • Local Network: Used exclusively for the WiFi Transfer feature, which allows you to transfer files from a browser on the same local network. This feature does not involve internet connectivity or cloud communication.
  • Notifications (optional): Used to deliver local device notifications when an audio conversion completes or encounters an error. This permission is optional — declining it does not affect any core functionality.

数据共享与第三方Data Sharing & Third Parties

音频随转不会出售、出租或以任何方式向第三方提供您的个人数据。本应用不含广告 SDK 或跨应用追踪。唯一的第三方服务是上文所述的 Google Analytics for Firebase 与 Firebase Crashlytics，可在设置中关闭。Google Analytics 中的用户与事件数据保留期设为 14 个月；关闭或卸载不会自动删除 Google 已接收的历史数据，如需删除请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

AudioTools does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal data with any third party. The app has no advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. The only third-party services are Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics as described above, which you can turn off in Settings. User and event data in Google Analytics is retained for 14 months; turning reporting off or uninstalling does not delete data Google has already received — contact us at the email below to request deletion.
