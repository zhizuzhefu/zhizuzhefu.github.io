upupfrog · iOS 17

Watch an estimate of today’s pay move

Enter a monthly salary and a schedule. upupfrog refreshes every tenth of a second from the current time, so leaving the app does not add leftover seconds by mistake. Your salary stays on this iPhone.

Two ways to read it

  • Demo mode spreads the month across every hour of the current calendar month.
  • Work mode uses your workdays, start and end hours, and a lunch break that starts at 12:00.
  • Fifteen currencies. The rate request does not include your salary. A built-in table is the fallback.
  • Eight example prices, or type your own. Price guide.

The arithmetic is in the salary guide.

Questions

Does upupfrog deposit money?

No. It estimates today’s earnings from the salary, hours, and currency you enter. It does not pay, transfer, or place an order.

What is the difference between Demo and Work mode?

Demo mode counts the whole 24-hour day. Work mode counts only your start-to-end window and pauses for the lunch block that begins at noon. The number is recalculated from the clock.

Do I need a subscription to see the counter?

No. The counter, price converter, and widget work without a subscription. A monthly or yearly subscription unlocks every theme and later Pro benefits. The price is the one shown on the App Store.
