Estimate · on device

From a monthly figure to this moment

Work mode divides your monthly amount by the workdays you enter, then by the seconds inside your start and end hours after lunch. Demo mode divides by the number of days in the current month and counts all 24 hours.

Lunch and the clock

In Work mode the lunch pause begins at 12:00 and lasts for the duration you set. Before the workday starts, today’s figure is zero. After the end hour, it stays at that day’s amount. The display refreshes every 0.1 seconds from the clock.

To turn a purchase into work time, open the price guide.

Questions

Is the on-screen number my actual paycheck?

No. upupfrog only estimates from what you type. It does not connect to a bank or send a payment.

Does backgrounding the app make the total drift?

No. Each refresh uses the current time and your settings. It does not add up the seconds the app spent in the background.
