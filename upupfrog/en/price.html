15 currencies

Turn a price into hours of work

upupfrog divides the amount by the pay rate from your settings. Sample items include a drink, takeout, a movie ticket, coffee, shoes, rent, and two larger electronics examples. They are illustrations, not shelf prices.

Currencies

CNY, USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, HKD, KRW, TWD, SGD, AUD, CAD, CHF, INR, THB, and VND. How the hourly rate is produced is explained in the salary guide.

Questions

Are the preset prices live store prices?

No. The drink, meal, ticket, shoes, and rent figures are sample yuan amounts stored in the app, then converted into the currency you selected. Type the price you actually care about.

Does the exchange-rate request include my salary?

No. The only network request fetches rates for 15 currencies. If it fails, upupfrog uses a built-in table. Your salary remains on the iPhone.
