隐私保护Privacy

涨涨蛙不收集任何个人信息。你的薪资数据仅存储在设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。

唯一的网络请求是获取汇率数据，用于 15 种货币换算；失败时使用内置汇率。请求不发送薪资。计数、换算和小组件不需要订阅。订阅只解锁全部主题，价格以 App Store 为准。说明见 实时工资 和 价格换算。

upupfrog does not collect any personal information. Your salary data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

The only network request fetches exchange rates for 15 currencies and does not send your salary. If it fails, a built-in table is used. The counter, price converter, and widget do not need a subscription. Monthly or yearly subscription unlocks all themes. The price is whatever the App Store shows.

常见问题Questions

涨涨蛙会把工资转进账户吗？Does upupfrog deposit money?

不会。它只根据你填写的月薪、工时和币种估算今天已经赚了多少，不处理支付、转账或任何真实交易。No. It estimates today's earnings from the salary, hours, and currency you enter. It does not pay, transfer, or place an order.

演示模式和工作模式有什么区别？What is the difference between Demo and Work mode?

演示模式按全天 24 小时持续估算。工作模式只在你设置的上下班时间内计算，并扣除午休。数字按当前时间重算，不是离开后再累加。Demo mode counts the whole 24-hour day. Work mode counts only your start-to-end window and pauses for the lunch block that begins at noon. The number is recalculated from the clock.

必须订阅才能看收入吗？Do I need a subscription to see the counter?

不用。计数、价格换算和桌面小组件不需要订阅。月付或年付订阅用于解锁全部主题，以及之后上线的 Pro 权益。价格以 App Store 显示为准。No. The counter, price converter, and widget work without a subscription. A monthly or yearly subscription unlocks every theme and later Pro benefits. The price is the one shown on the App Store.
