把月薪变成此刻的一个数字From a monthly figure to this moment

填上月薪、币种、上下班时间、午休和每月工作天数。涨涨蛙每 0.1 秒刷新一次，让你看见今天已经过去的那一部分大约值多少钱。它是估算器，不是工资条。Work mode divides your monthly amount by the workdays you enter, then by the seconds inside your start and end hours after lunch. Demo mode divides by the number of days in the current month and counts all 24 hours.

两种算法Two ways to read it

  • 演示模式按全天 24 小时估算。日薪用月薪除以这个自然月的天数。Demo mode spreads the month across every hour of the current calendar month.
  • 工作模式只计算你设置的上下班时间，每月按你填的工作天数分摊月薪。Work mode uses your workdays, start and end hours, and a lunch break that starts at 12:00.
  • 工作模式的午休从中午 12 点开始，按你设置的时长扣除。午休期间数字停住。In Work mode the lunch pause begins at 12:00 and lasts for the duration you set. Before the workday starts, today's figure is zero.
  • 还没到上班时间，今天的数字是 0。下班之后停在当天的日薪。After the end hour, the number stays at that day's amount. The display refreshes every 0.1 seconds from the clock.

薪资留在这台手机Your salary stays on this phone

月薪写在本机。获取汇率时不会把薪资发出去。计数、换算和桌面小组件不需要订阅。月付或年付订阅只解锁全部主题，以及之后上线的 Pro 权益，价格以 App Store 显示为准。The monthly amount is stored locally. The rate request does not include your salary; a built-in table is the fallback. 常见问题Questions

这个数字会打进工资卡吗？Is the on-screen number my actual paycheck?

不会。涨涨蛙只在手机上估算，不连接银行，也不处理发薪、支付或转账。No. upupfrog only estimates from what you type. It does not connect to a bank or send a payment.

切到后台之后数字会算错吗？Does backgrounding the app make the total drift?

不会按离开的秒数往上加。它每次都用现在的钟点，按你的月薪和工时重新计算今天已经过去的有效时间。No. Each refresh uses the current time and your settings. It does not add up the seconds the app spent in the background.
