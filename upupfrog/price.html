时薪 · 15 种货币Hourly rate · 15 currencies

看看一件东西要上班多久Turn a price into hours of work

涨涨蛙用你已经设置的收入速度，把一个价格换成工作时间。可以点示例，也可以自己输入。它不帮你下单，也不比较商家的实时价格。upupfrog divides the amount by the pay rate from your settings. Sample items include a drink, takeout, a movie ticket, coffee, shoes, rent, and two larger electronics examples. They are illustrations, not shelf prices.

示例和自己的价格Samples and your own price

App 里有 8 个人民币示例：奶茶 25、外卖 35、电影票 50、咖啡 38、鞋 1299、房租 3000，以及两档更高的电子产品示例。选了别的货币之后，这些数字会按汇率换算，方便你看「大约要工作多久」，不是商店标签。Eight sample yuan amounts are stored in the app — a drink, takeout, a movie ticket, coffee, shoes, rent, and two electronics tiers. Pick another currency and they convert by the current rate so you can see roughly how long you would work — they are not shelf prices.

支持人民币、美元、欧元、日元、英镑、港币、韩元、新台币、新加坡元、澳元、加元、瑞士法郎、印度卢比、泰铢和越南盾。工资怎么算，见 实时工资。CNY, USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, HKD, KRW, TWD, SGD, AUD, CAD, CHF, INR, THB, and VND. How the hourly rate is produced is explained in the salary guide.

常见问题Questions

示例里的价格是商品标价吗？Are the preset prices live store prices?

不是。奶茶、外卖、电影票、咖啡、鞋和房租这些数字是写在 App 里的人民币示例，再按当前汇率换成你选的货币。真正要算的价格请自己输入。No. The drink, meal, ticket, shoes, and rent figures are sample yuan amounts stored in the app, then converted into the currency you selected. Type the price you actually care about.

汇率请求会带上我的工资吗？Does the exchange-rate request include my salary?

不会。唯一的网络请求只取 15 种货币的汇率。失败时使用内置汇率表。薪资仍只存在这台 iPhone 上。No. The only network request fetches rates for 15 currencies. If it fails, upupfrog uses a built-in table. Your salary remains on the iPhone.
